## Scope

Workflow detail screen and repository requirement submission. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience and task

Individual developers and reviewers inspect an AI-assisted repository workflow, understand what is happening now, review outputs, and decide the next action. While an AI or verification request is pending, make the current phase and elapsed wait unmistakable.

## Constraints

Preserve workflow actions, authorization, persisted workflow behavior, AI validation, and human approval boundaries. Keep navigation and controls familiar. The previous category-standard visual direction felt too bland; the user asks for an original, authentic, stylish interface. Do not imply streaming tokens or subtask progress when the server does not provide it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Changeweft turns an opaque AI delivery process into a legible route from requirement to reviewed change; every station records where evidence enters and where a human takes over.

OWN-WORLD: Use midnight blue as the shell ground with porcelain text and four route inks—scarlet, cobalt, amber, and green—assigned consistently to analysis, planning, implementation, and verification. Keep standard sans-serif web controls and crisp, fixed-size labels. Color has a status meaning across every screen.

STORY: The visitor follows one connected route, sees the current AI station and elapsed time, then reviews the evidence and takes the next available action. Human decisions are explicit transfer points; generated changes remain drafts until approval.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the repository breadcrumb, workflow title, and requirement at the top. Directly beneath, show a horizontal responsive route diagram with five labeled stops and a bright current-station marker. Follow it with the current step, elapsed time, and a concise explanation; analysis and plan details continue below in the established workflow order.

FORM: Midnight transit-diagram wayfinding, challenger 4 from the bolder reroll, seed fddef786. Chosen for stronger audience identification and product clarity than the abstract data-sublime leader; route-lines and transfer points map to actual workflow progression and human approval.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
